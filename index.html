How to play

1. Read the requests. The audience tells you what they want to hear (top-left of the stage).

2. Program your robots. Pick a robot in the list on the left (or click it on stage). Melodic robots get the big pitch grid: click or drag to place notes; higher rows are higher notes, shaded rows are the "home" note. Drum and percussion robots get one lane each under the grid.

3. Perform! Press Space or ▶. When every request is met, play the loop all the way through to fill the meter and earn a standing ovation.

Tips: 🎲 Riff writes a pattern for the selected robot. 💡 on a request explains it. Change a robot's octave and start note in its card. Ctrl+Z undoes. Drag the stage to look around.
